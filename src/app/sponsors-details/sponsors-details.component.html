Srini Nannapaneni's Generous Contribution to Empower Program in his Village

Srini Nannapaneni, an esteemed Non-Resident Indian (NRI), has demonstrated an inspiring act of generosity by contributing a remarkable sum of ₹15 lakh to his native village. This contribution is dedicated to the Empower Program, a transformative initiative aimed at uplifting the local community through education, skill development, and infrastructure enhancement. Hailing from the village, Srini has always maintained a deep connection to his roots, despite living abroad. His philanthropic gesture reflects his commitment to giving back to the community that played a significant role in shaping his early life.
